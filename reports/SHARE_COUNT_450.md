The guard is independent of the reader and of any hand reading: a pre-issue count times the final offer price must imply a market capitalisation of roughly 1x to 25x the money raised, because an issue is a slice of a company. A count ten or a hundred times wrong fails it. That makes the pass rate evidence about the reader at scale, rather than an extrapolation from the three hand-read cases it was graded on.
